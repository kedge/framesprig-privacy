FrameSprig Privacy Policy

Updated: 6 November 2026

FrameSprig is an Android photo organizer developed by kedge. Its package name is com.kedge.framesprig.

Photos and local settings

With your Android permission, FrameSprig reads locally accessible photos and videos and related file details to help you review, sort, and find duplicate or similar images. The organizer processes this information on your device. Its private local database stores a media index, content fingerprints, review selections, session progress and trash operation results. Preview images use a bounded memory cache. The organizer does not upload your photos, thumbnails, file names, locations in photos, or photo analysis to kedge or InMobi. FrameSprig does not require a developer account or sign-in. Android backup and device transfer of the app's private organizer data are disabled.

Optional advertising

Builds configured for optional ads can use the InMobi SDK. Ads are off by default. When ads are enabled and loaded, InMobi may process information such as your IP address, advertising identifier, device and app information, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, secure, and prevent abuse of ads. InMobi handles this information according to its own privacy policy. You can turn optional ads off in FrameSprig settings and manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Turning ads off stops new ad requests from FrameSprig and does not delete information previously processed by InMobi.

When configured, InMobi CMP presents applicable privacy choices and stores consent signals on the device. Loading the CMP involves communication with InMobi to retrieve the privacy configuration and applicable choices. The advertising SDK is gated until that process has provided the required signals. You can reopen privacy choices from the optional advertising setting. Pro disables advertising in the app.

Optional Pro purchases

The app includes a seven-day full-feature trial starting at first launch. The trial start and latest observed time are stored locally to determine trial availability; no trial account or trial device identifier is sent to the developer. There is no automatic charge when the trial ends. A one-time Google Play purchase is required to start new sorting after the trial. Existing review lists, undo and restoration of available trash items remain accessible.

Builds configured for purchases use Google Play Billing for a one-time Pro product. Google processes the payment; FrameSprig does not receive card information. A purchase token is sent over HTTPS to the developer's verification service, which checks the purchase and acknowledges it with Google Play. The verification service does not receive photos or retain or log purchase tokens or order details. Hosting and network providers may process connection information needed to operate the service. The app caches a verified entitlement for up to seven days and queries Google Play when returning to the foreground. Purchase and refund records held by Google follow Google's retention and privacy policies.

Optional reminders

If enabled, a local reminder is scheduled once a week. Android notification permission is requested where required. Reminder content does not include photo names or images. You can disable reminders in the app or Android settings.

Control and retention

You can change or revoke photo access in Android settings. Clear FrameSprig’s app storage or uninstall it to remove its local review records and preferences. Photos in the Android media library and trash follow the device’s own retention and restoration rules. FrameSprig asks Android to move selected items to trash only after you review and confirm them.

The in-app Clear photo index action removes scan results and preview caches while preserving your review choices; it does not delete original photos. Removing all local app data also resets local preferences and review history. A Google Play purchase can be restored through Google Play when purchase verification is available. Demo mode uses included sample images and does not modify your media library.
